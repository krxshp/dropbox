# dropbox
A streamlined dropbox system designed to simplify guest checkouts by enabling rapid key-card deactivation, overnight returns, and significantly shorter front-desk lines. The pre-alpha prototype was submitted for approval by university campus leadership for a conference season serving more than 4,000 beds.

[Demo Video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_QEnrg8nC1vaeK2B4mKuVnDyp_8ror7V/view?usp=sharing)

## Hardware
- 1x Intel Mini PC
- 1x ESP32 Dev board
- 1x IR Sensor
- 1x Relay
- 2x Magnetic locks
- 1x DC Power Supply
- Dropbox case (used custom wooden box)

## Software
- Python
- C++

## Notes
- The Intel Mini PC was selected to maintain compatibility with the university’s restricted Windows-based systems. The initial prototype used a Raspberry Pi 5, which was soon replaced.
- The ESP32 detects deposited cards using the IR sensor and controls the magnetic locks through a relay. With the card tap and detected card drop, two emails are sent to front desk staff and the tap log is updated.
- The project remained in pending approval stage before being handed off to next co-ops.
